Employee Onboarding Program

Challenge

New hires took longer than expected to reach full productivity; onboarding quality varied by department.

Approach

Designed a structured onboarding journey with culture orientation, role-specific training, and scheduled milestone check-ins.

Sample — Onboarding Milestone Map
Day 1

Culture orientation, workstation setup, buddy assigned

Week 1

Role-specific training, systems access, team intros

Day 30

Manager check-in, feedback survey

Day 60

Skill-gap review, goal alignment

Day 90

Performance check-in, confirmation

Why it matters — Consistent onboarding shortens ramp-up time and gives new hires a clear path to full productivity from day one.

KPI & Performance Management Framework

Challenge

Approach

Designed a KPI-driven performance system aligned to departmental goals, with a structured quarterly review cadence.

Sample — KPI Framework Structure
CategorySample KPIFrequency
